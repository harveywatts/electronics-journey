# 01 - First LED

A couple of days ago (08 August 2026) marked the very beginning of my electronics journey. I successfully wired my first LED and programmed it to blink on and off every 1 second using basic Arduino code.

I then experimented with the code, increasing and decreasing the intervals between each blink.

I used an Arduino Uno R3, an LED, a resistor, breadboard, jumper wires and my ThinkPad as the power source.

This may have been a very simple circuit, but this was an important milestone for me as it was a first.

## What I learned:
- How a breadboard works
- How to connect an LED safely
- Why a resistor is needed with an LED
